# Join split payments on the customer statement

- [ ] List every payment once, newest first, with its amount, method and the debts it paid.
- [ ] A payment the shop split over several debts shows as one row whose amount is the sum of its parts, marked as split.
- [ ] A payment that paid a single debt is never marked as split, including a split payment whose other debts are no longer on the statement (cancelled debts are left out).
